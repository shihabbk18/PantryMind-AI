A TRANSPARENT KITCHEN COMPANION

Useful, private, and honest.

PantryMind runs in your browser. Your photos, pantry, favorites, and meal history stay on this device. No paid AI API, backend, account, or cloud synchronization is required.

Photo confirmation

Automatic dish recognition is not included in this build. Photos are a visual reference. Recipe templates suggest likely ingredients; you confirm the names and quantities before any nutrition calculation.

Nutrition data

50 records from USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy, per 100 g of the stated raw or cooked edible food. Every ingredient includes its FDC ID in the source dataset. Values are estimates, not personalized dietary advice.

Meal recommendations

Recipes are authored bundled templates. They are ranked by quantity coverage, missing ingredients, and preparation time. They are not generated by a language model. Dietary labels describe listed ingredients; they do not certify allergens or packaged products.

Recipe photographs

Bundled Unsplash-licensed food photos are serving suggestions, not newly AI-generated images. Photo sources.

Offline use

After the first visit and successful offline cache setup, core features work without internet. Some private browsing modes restrict storage. Mobile camera availability depends on the browser and permissions.
