A TRANSPARENT KITCHEN COMPANION

Useful, private, and honest.

PantryMind runs in your browser. Your photos, pantry, favorites, and meal history stay on this device. No paid AI API, backend, account, or cloud synchronization is required.

Photo confirmation

A free Food-101 classifier runs locally. Its probable dish selects an ingredient template and standard serving assumptions. These are not measured weights or verified ingredients. Nutrition is calculated from USDA records. Recognition can miss mixed dishes and non-food images; optional edits improve estimates.

Nutrition data

50 records from USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy, per 100 g of the stated raw or cooked edible food. Every ingredient includes its FDC ID in the source dataset. Values are estimates, not personalized dietary advice.

Meal recommendations

Recipes are authored bundled templates. Names-only entries are ranked by ingredient overlap; optional measured entries use quantity coverage. Nutrition uses standard recipe serving weights. They are not generated by a language model. Dietary labels describe listed ingredients; they do not certify allergens or packaged products.

Recipe photographs

Bundled Unsplash-licensed food photos are serving suggestions, not newly AI-generated images. Photo sources.

Offline use

After successful cache setup, manual nutrition and pantry features work offline. Recognition additionally needs a first model download from Hugging Face (no photo upload or hosted inference); cached weights may be evicted by your browser. Some private browsing modes restrict storage. Mobile camera availability depends on the browser and permissions.
